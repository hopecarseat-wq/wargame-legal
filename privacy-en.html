그냥만든전쟁게임 (Wargame) Privacy Policy

Written: 2026-11-18 / Effective: the official release date on Google Play

Operator: Kim Chungyeom (김춘겸) (Privacy officer: Kim Chungyeom)

Privacy contact email: hopecarseat@gmail.com

Public URL: https://api.kcgwargame.com/privacy

This document describes what the app and game server actually process today.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the "그냥만든전쟁게임" (Wargame) app (com.hopec.wargame) and the game server it connects to.

2. Account (guest account)

  • When you first open the app, a guest account is created without sign-up. The app sends a random device number it generates (not a hardware identifier), an account secret, the platform (e.g. android) and the app version to the server. The server stores the secret only as a hash.
  • The server gives each account an 8-digit public number (in-game ID) and a nickname. Your nickname may be visible to other players.
  • The app currently has no Google account linking. If we add it, we will store the account identifier Google provides (only if you choose to link) and update this policy.

    • 3. What the server stores

  • Progress: gold and skip tickets, owned units and facilities and their upgrades, decks (formations and facility placement), home city.
  • Mail and rewards: rewards and mail you received.
  • Match records: Defense and Conquest match results and participation.
  • Friends: friend and block lists, friend request records.
  • Operations records: gold and holdings change ledger (reason, request number, IP address of the request), suspicious-activity records (including IP address), operator action records.
  • Access log: time of access, app version, device model name (the name the app sends, or browser information if none), country of access (only the country code derived from the IP address — the access log does not keep the IP address).
  • Payments: the app currently has no paid purchases. When purchases open, we store the Google Play purchase token, order number, product and processing result.

    • 4. Advertising (Google AdMob rewarded ads)

  • The app uses only Google AdMob rewarded ads. An ad is shown only when you choose "Watch an ad for ×3 gold" after a Defense match; there are no forced ads.
  • To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK (Google) collects and sends to Google the advertising ID (such as the Android advertising ID), device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language), IP address, ad interactions (ads shown, whether watched to the end) and approximate location (based on IP). Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  • The ad reward (×3 gold) is granted only after the game server verifies it. For this, the ad carries your in-game account number and the match number, and the server keeps the viewing confirmation (transaction ID and reward information) that Google sends.
  • In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, we first ask with Google's consent form (UMP); you get personalized or non-personalized ads based on your choice. You can change your ad consent in Settings (in those regions).
  • You can reset the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone settings.

    • 5. Purposes

    Verifying your account and keeping you signed in, saving progress and continuing on other devices, running matches, preventing abuse, handling support requests, and keeping the service stable.

    6. Sharing, processors and international transfer

  • We do not sell personal information. The only data that goes to another company is what the ad SDK (Google AdMob) collects, as described in section 4.
  • The game server is operated by the operator in the Republic of Korea. Connections go through the network of Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) (Cloudflare Tunnel), which also manages the domain (kcgwargame.com).
  • The country code comes from the network service in front of the server (Cloudflare).

    • 7. Retention

  • Account and progress: until the account is deleted.
  • When you request account deletion, it is deleted after 7 days (section 9).
  • Payment records (once paid items are introduced): kept for 5 years as required by the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (they remain after account deletion).
  • Access logs, operations records and suspicious-activity records: there is currently no automatic deletion period; they are deleted, or their identifying part (IP address) is cleared, when the account is deleted.

    • 8. Your rights

    You can ask to view, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing, by emailing the contact above. Please include your in-game ID (Settings → My ID). The privacy officer (Kim Chungyeom) will respond within 10 days of receiving the request.

    9. How to request account deletion

  • In the app: Settings → [Delete account] → [Request deletion]. After 7 days your nickname (→ "Deleted account"), linked accounts, access log, decks, rewards and mail, match records, friends and progress are deleted and cannot be restored. IP addresses in the gold ledger and suspicious-activity records are also cleared. You can cancel in the same place within 7 days.
  • If you cannot use the app: email the contact above with your in-game ID and we will process it the same way (deleted 7 days after the request).
  • Deleting the app or its data on your phone does not delete the server account. Please use the methods above.
  • Payment records remain after deletion because the law requires them to be kept.

    • 10. Age and changes

    Target age: 15 and over. The Service is not intended for children under 14; if we learn we hold information of a child under 14, we will delete it. If this policy changes, we will announce the changes and the effective date on the public URL and in the app.
